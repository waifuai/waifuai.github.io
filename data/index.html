Decision details & estimates

Before doing heavier work — drawing a picture, changing a scene, picking an emotion or a voice — the companions ask a small decision model what to do. Confident “nothing to do” answers let the app skip the heavier call entirely: 4,949 of 29,036 answers so far (17%).
