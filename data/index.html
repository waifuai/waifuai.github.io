Inside the companion

Quiet decisions, faster companions.#

Before doing heavier work — drawing a picture, changing a scene, picking an emotion or a voice — the companions ask a small decision model what to do. Confident “nothing to do” answers let the app skip the heavier call entirely: 50 of 206 answers so far (24%).
