  • The only observed same-index v5 light-line step is the +23.1 whole-generation jump (3.8 Flash 30.8 → Hydrogen 53.9); applied 1:1 it would project about 77, above Fable 5.2 (65) — moderated, so the +2 stays a conservative labeled scenario over the Hydrogen anchor (outlier moderated; independent estimate, not a benchmark)
