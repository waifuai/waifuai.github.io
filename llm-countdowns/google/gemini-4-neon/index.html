Why this date

  • Google shipped Gemini 4 Hydrogen (gemini-4-hydrogen) on Nov 12, 2026 as the element-named everyday/execution tier: GA in AI Studio and the Gemini API, 1M-token context, $1.50/$7.50 per 1M, a fourth 'xhigh' thinking level, the default model in Antigravity and the upgrade path from Gemini 3.8 Flash in Google Search
  • The Flash/Flash-Lite names end with the Gemini 3 generation: the Oct-Nov changelog adds no Flash or Flash-Lite model, and the Nov 2 entry still routes gemini-2.5-flash to gemini-3.5-flash-lite from Dec 1 — the element series (Argon, Carbon, Hydrogen) is the active naming, with Neon the only additional element name on record
  • Neon is unannounced: the only trace is a gemini-4-neon string in the Gemini web app's code reported Nov 18, 2026; community reads make it a faster/lighter variant (possibly the budget slot, in which case it succeeds 3.5 Flash-Lite rather than Hydrogen and could land lower), and no specs, tier or date exist
  • The Dec 10 target is an owner-requested ~4-week buffer (Nov 12 sweep convention) counted from Hydrogen's Nov 12 release, not an announced date; if Neon is the budget slot it may wait for the 9-11-week gap Lite historically kept behind its Flash
  • The Nov 19 AA catalog reads Gemini 4 Hydrogen at 53.9 on the v5 index (default row; High 50.8, Medium 41.2, Low 26.9) versus Gemini 3.8 Flash at 30.8 — a +23.1 whole-generation step that, applied 1:1, would project about 77, above Fable 5.2 (65); implausible for the next light-tier model, so the +2 stays a conservative labeled scenario over the measured Hydrogen anchor (outlier moderated; independent estimate, not a benchmark)
