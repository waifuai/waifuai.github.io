Why this date

  • Flash-Lite always ships after the Flash of the same generation: 2.0 (20 days later), 2.5 (4 weeks), 3.1 (11 weeks after 3 Flash) and 3.5 (9 weeks after 3.5 Flash)
  • Lite updates far less often than Flash: there was no 3.6, 3.7 or 3.8 Flash-Lite, so the next one probably waits for the Gemini 4 generation
  • The date counts about two months from the Gemini 4 Flash forecast (mid Nov), pushed past the holidays; if Gemini 4 Flash slips, this slips too
  • Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite (22.2, $0.30/$2.50, about 340 tokens/s) is Google's fastest model; each Lite generation has gained 6 to 7 points, so the projection assumes about 30
