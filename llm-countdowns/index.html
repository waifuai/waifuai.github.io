How these estimates work

Each date combines what a lab has said publicly, credible reporting, and how often that lab has shipped before. When a lab gives no signal, the date follows its usual release cycle.

High confidence means the lab or strong reporting points at the window. Medium confidence is an informed guess from cadence and indirect signals. Speculative has little to go on.
