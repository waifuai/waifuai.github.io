Why this date

  • Alibaba's Model Studio docs list qwen4-max-preview, in preview from 10:00 on Nov 11 to 24:00 on Nov 25, 2026 (UTC+8), available through Token Plan only, with a 1M-token context window and 393,216 max output. The Sept 22 announcement had said Qwen 4 was in training.
  • Qwen's official X post (Nov 11) says 'Qwen4 is coming... Qwen4-Max is now in preview', with early access on QwenWork, Qoder, Qwen APP and QwenStudio. A preview is not a stable Max release, and no GA date is announced.
  • Qoder's official notice checked Nov 12 still says Qoduck is a stealth model whose identity has yet to be disclosed. Its Nov 7-11 free-allowance promotion (Singapore time) is not a verified Qwen release date, and Qoder's preview early access does not disclose Qoduck's identity.
  • Qwen Max has updated every 1 to 3 months (3.7 Max May 19, 3.8 Max Aug 3, 3.8 Max 0902 Sept 2); the Dec 10 target is an owner-requested ~4-week buffer (Nov 12), not a newly confirmed schedule. Alibaba named Qwen 4.5 and Qwen 5 as upcoming roadmap series; neither is established as the exact next public model.
  • The Qwen3.8 Max (0902) baseline of 33.6 is unchanged. Qwen3.7 Max and Qwen3.6 Max Preview have no v5 score in the Nov 12 AA API catalog, so no same-index v5 delta exists. Launch coverage reports the 0803->0902 refresh at 45 vs 40 on v4.3 (+5, +12.5%); scaling that onto the retained 33.6 anchor gives +4.2, projecting about 37.8 (scaled from v4.3 - scenario, not a v5 benchmark). A full major-generation step (Qwen3.8 Max 0803 +11 over Qwen3.7 Max, 47->58 on v4.1.1, +23.4%) would scale to about +7.9; the conservative refresh floor is retained pending a confirmed Qwen 4 Max GA.
