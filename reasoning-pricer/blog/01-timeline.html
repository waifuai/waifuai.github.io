The AI Timeline: Modeling Asset Value in an Accelerated Future

In the rapidly evolving landscape of 2026 and beyond, traditional valuation models are failing. The key insight driving the Reasoning Pricer is simple yet profound: AI progress affects different asset types differently.

As Artificial Intelligence accelerates, we observe a financial divergence: - Static assets (like BTC, gold, and fiat-pegged stablecoins) decline in relative value as they cannot adapt. - AI-evolving assets (utility tokens, AI-native protocols) appreciate exponentially as they leverage AI for sustained evolution and efficiency.

The Timeline Phases

We model this acceleration through a series of phases, each representing a step-change in AI capability and its economic impact:

Phase Timeframe AI Capability Market Impact
Personal Architect 2025 Q2 Scientific agents; AI as personal architect First divergence; hard money begins losing relative ground.
Global Acceleration Accord 2025 Q4 AI acceleration frameworks Protocol utility draws level with AI-native; static assets fall behind.
Creative Renaissance 2026 AI as creative partner Peak acceleration; AI-native assets see massive adoption. This is the phase the current reports price against.
Agentic (Waifu Aligner) 2027 Autonomous AI agents Full ecosystem integration; autonomous agents become primary economic actors.

AI Impact by Asset Type

The model tracks three multiplier curves across the phases above. These are the actual constants in AI_TIMELINE_PHASES, showing the divergence from the 2024 pre-AI baseline through the Agentic phase in 2027:

AI-Native rises 16.7x off its baseline while Hard Money falls to 8% of its own — a 200x relative swing between the two over three years. Asset classes that are not on one of these curves (liquid staking, real yield, fiat-pegged stablecoins, memes) get their valuation from a fixed base-multiplier range instead, scaled by the AI category their tags resolve to. Fiat-pegged assets sit at a 0.01x - 0.05x base and take the Static 0.20x penalty on top, which is why they land four orders of magnitude below the protocol tokens.

The Valuation Formula

This timeline is not just theoretical—it is encoded directly into the Reasoning Pricer engine. The "Real Multiplier" for any given token is calculated as:

Real Multiplier = Base_Multiplier × AI_Timeline_Factor × Risk_Factors

By dynamically adjusting the AI_Timeline_Factor based on the current acceleration phase and the token's specific AI Category (e.g., Static, PassiveUtility, or AINative), the model provides a forward-looking valuation that accounts for the technological singularity.
