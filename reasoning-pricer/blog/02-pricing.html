The Mathematics of Predicted Price and Tariff Rates

How does the Reasoning Pricer determine the real value of a token in an AI-accelerating economy? This post breaks down the core formulas that power the system.

The Predicted Price Formula

The Predicted Price represents what a token should be worth after adjusting for AI acceleration. It's calculated as:

Predicted Price = Current Price × Real Valuation Multiplier

The Real Valuation Multiplier is the heart of the system—a composite score that captures a token's true worth:

Real Valuation Multiplier = Base_Type × AI_Timeline × Risk_Class × Insider_Risk × Exchange_Multiplier × Capital_Flight × Liquidity_Risk

where Exchange_Multiplier = 1 + (tariff_override / 100) — the analyst-set friction premium from the token's data file. It is easy to miss, but it is a real factor in the product: SOL carries a tariff_override of 30, so its multiplier is scaled by 1.30x, and Wormhole's override of 300 scales W by 4.00x.

Let's examine each component.

The Base Multiplier is the midpoint of each range:

Base_Multiplier = (Min_Range + Max_Range) / 2

A token whose token_type is missing or unrecognised falls back to a 5x - 15x Protocol Utility range rather than the 10x - 25x above.

Component 2: AI Timeline Factor

This is the key innovation—different assets respond differently to AI progress.

The repository contains two engines, and they read the timeline differently. tariff_generator.py — which produces the predicted-price, tariff-rate, and master-table reports — looks the factor up from a flat table calibrated to the current phase. pricing_engine.py — which produces the per-token reports under reports/tokens/ — instead derives it as a ratio:

AI_Timeline_Factor = Current_Phase_Multiplier / Baseline_Multiplier

Component 4: Other Risk Factors

Insider Risk Factor (0.5 to 1.0): Insider_Risk = 1.0 - (Insider_Score / 100) × 0.5 Higher insider control = higher centralization risk.

Capital Flight Factor: Based on market cap rank. - Rank < 10: 1.2x (blue-chip premium) - Rank < 50: 1.0x (neutral) - Rank < 100: 0.8x (slight penalty) - Rank < 300: 0.5x (moderate penalty) - Rank ≥ 300: 0.2x (heavy penalty for illiquid alts)

A token with no rank in its data file is treated as rank 500, landing it in the 0.2x band.

Liquidity Risk Factor: SOL is exempt; others receive 0.90x - 1.0x based on market cap.

The Tariff Rate Formula

The Tariff Rate represents transaction friction—a tax on moving between assets. Higher-value assets have lower tariffs.

Base Tariff Calculation

Base_Tariff = max(0, (100 / Real_Valuation_Multiplier) - 10)

This formula ensures: - 10x multiplier → 0% tariff (baseline) - 5x multiplier → 10% tariff - 1x multiplier → 90% tariff - 0.1x multiplier → 990% tariff

Effective Tariff (with Liquidity Penalty)

Non-SOL tokens receive an additional liquidity penalty:

Effective_Tariff = Base_Tariff + Liquidity_Penalty

Market Cap Liquidity Penalty
≥ $1B +10%
≥ $100M +15%
≥ $10M +20%
< $10M +25%

SOL is exempt from the liquidity penalty as the native liquidity hub.

Exchange Price Formula

The Exchange Price represents immediate purchasing power after tariff friction:

Exchange_Price = Current_Price / (1 + Tariff_Rate / 100)

Example: A token with 100% tariff: - Current Price: $10.00 - Exchange Price: $10.00 / (1 + 100/100) = $10.00 / 2.0 = $5.00

Worked Example: RENDER vs USDC

Both worked through against the August 2026 data snapshot, showing every factor in the product.

RENDER (AI-Enabled Token)

Factor Value Source
Base Type 17.5x Protocol Utility midpoint of 10x - 25x
AI Timeline 5.00x AI-Enabled
Risk Class 1.2x Class A (Real Yield)
Insider Risk 0.825x insider score 35
Exchange Multiplier 1.15x tariff_override: 15
Capital Flight 0.8x rank 50
Liquidity Risk 0.95x market cap $663M (under $1B)

17.5 × 5.00 × 1.2 × 0.825 × 1.15 × 0.8 × 0.95 = 75.71x

  • Real Multiplier: 75.71x
  • Base Tariff: max(0, 100/75.71 - 10) = 0% — the multiplier is far past the 10x break-even, so the raw term is negative and clamps to zero
  • Liquidity Penalty: +15% (market cap in the $100M - $1B band)
  • Effective Tariff: 15%
  • Predicted Price: $1.28 × 75.71 = $96.91

Note that RENDER's multiplier fell from 79.7x to 75.7x in this refresh with no model change at all. Its market cap dropped below $1B, which flipped the liquidity risk factor from 1.0x to 0.95x.

The blue-chip rank premium and the deep liquidity that USDC earns for being a top-10 asset are the only things holding the multiplier up. They buy it a 1.2x and a 1.0x against a 0.20x static penalty applied to a 0.03x base — the rank premium is rounding error next to the asset-class verdict.

The math reveals the fundamental truth: AI-native assets gain value as AI accelerates, while static assets—especially fiat-pegged—collapse.

Key Takeaways

  1. Predicted Price = Current Price × Real Valuation Multiplier
  2. Multiplier is a composite of seven factors: type, AI timeline, risk class, insider risk, the analyst tariff_override, capital flight, and liquidity
  3. Tariff Rate = max(0, 100/Multiplier - 10) + liquidity penalty
  4. Exchange Price = Current Price / (1 + Tariff/100)
  5. The AI Timeline Factor is the differentiating variable—it's what separates the 76.6x top of the table (GRASS) from the 0.00095x bottom (BRZ), a spread of five orders of magnitude
